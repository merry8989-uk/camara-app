Camara.

A camera app that actually edits. 75+ filters, AR lenses, face-only beauty, real portrait depth, a teleprompter, and full photo and video editors — all on device.

Android 7.0+ ~5 MB On-device processing No account needed
Download the latest APK Source on GitHub

Install

  1. Open the latest release and download the .apk.
  2. Tap it. Android will ask you to allow Install unknown apps for your browser or file manager — allow it, then install.
  3. Grant camera and microphone access when asked. Without camera access the app cannot open; the permission screen has an Open app settings button if you deny it twice.

Updates install straight over the current version — no uninstall. You can also update from inside the app: Settings → Updates → Check for updates.

Camera

The capture screen is the app's home. Everything below is reachable from it.

Photo and video modes

Switch between still capture and video recording. Video records with audio when the microphone permission is granted, and silently when it is not.

Front / rear camera

One tap to switch lenses. Front-camera photos can be saved mirrored the way you see them, controlled from Settings.

Flash

Off, auto, or always on.

Timer

Off, 3, 5 or 10 seconds, with a visible countdown before the shutter fires. The default is set in Settings.

Composition grid

A rule-of-thirds overlay, toggleable, and optionally on by default.

Pinch to zoom

Continuous zoom with a live level indicator.

Tap to focus

Tap anywhere on the preview to focus and meter there. In Portrait mode a tap picks the subject instead.

Gallery shortcut

Jump straight to the last thing you shot.

Filters

Around 75 filters, grouped into categories so you can find one without scrolling forever.

  • See it before you pick it — every filter chip shows that filter applied to a real photograph, so you can tell what it does at a glance instead of trying each one. In the photo editor the chips use your own photo.
  • Categories — Original, Portrait, Classic, Warm, Cool, Mono, Cinematic, Vintage, Snap, Social. The category strip stays where you put it.
  • Strength slider — every filter blends from untouched to full, so you can dial an effect back instead of abandoning it.
  • Live preview — what you see on screen is what gets captured.
  • Remote catalogue — the filter list is synced from this repository, so new filters arrive without an app update. Settings → the sync button on the camera top bar pulls the latest.

AR lenses

Face-tracked effects that follow your head, anchored to real landmarks rather than floating in a fixed spot.

  • Landmark anchoring — effects scale with the distance between your eyes and rotate with your eye line, so they sit correctly instead of sliding around.
  • 28+ lenses across styles, from subtle to silly.
  • Honest about limits — if a lens can't run in the current camera mode, or the models behind it aren't available on your device, the app says so rather than silently doing nothing.

Beauty and retouch

Beauty applies to the face only — skin smoothing and skin-tone work through a feathered face mask, so the background, clothes and hair are untouched.

  • Smooth — softens skin texture without flattening detail.
  • Brighten — lifts the face out of shadow.
  • Warmth — warmer or cooler skin tone.
  • Skin tone — shifts the face's colour balance.
  • Slim face — a gentle horizontal squeeze inside the face oval.
  • Enlarge eyes — a localised, symmetric eye magnification.
  • Sharpen — brings back detail after smoothing.

Portrait depth

Real background blur with a subject you choose — not a fixed vignette.

  • Tap to select — tap the thing you want sharp; everything else blurs.
  • Works on people, pets and objects, not just faces.
  • Adjustable strength, and it is baked into the saved photo, not just the preview.

Stickers

Place stickers on the live camera preview, then drag and resize them. They are composited into the photo you save.

Teleprompter

A transparent, draggable script overlay that sits on the camera screen, so you can read it while looking at yourself and stay on camera.

  • Adjustable scroll speed and text size.
  • Transparent — you can see yourself through it while recording.
  • Draggable — put it wherever it doesn't block your framing.
  • Also available full-screen, for reading away from the camera.

Photo editor

Open anything from the gallery and edit it properly.

Crop

Freeform crop with draggable corners.

Rotate and flip

90° rotation plus horizontal and vertical flips.

Adjust

Brightness, contrast, saturation, warmth and more.

Filters

The same catalogue as the camera, with the strength slider.

Text

Add captions and move them into place.

Draw

Scribble on the photo with a colour picker.

Frames

Borders and edges to finish a shot.

Portrait depth

Tap to pick a subject and blur the rest, after the fact.

Undo and redo

Take back any change. A slider drag undoes in one step rather than a hundred.

Video editor

  • Trim — set the start and end of the clip.
  • Speed — slow motion and time-lapse, with per-segment speed changes.
  • Filters — grade the footage with the same catalogue.
  • Text — add captions and titles, then tap and drag them into place on the preview. Pick the size, the colour, and when each one shows, so a title can appear only at the start.
  • Stickers — drop emoji stickers on the video, position and resize them.
  • Undo and redo — take back a trim, filter, speed or overlay change.
  • Export — renders to your gallery with the text and stickers baked in, then share it straight from the app.

Gallery

Your photos and videos in a fast grid, with one-tap edit and share. Read access is requested only for the media permissions your Android version requires.

Settings

Every option persists and takes effect immediately. No dead toggles.

Appearance

  • Theme — light, dark, or follow the system.
  • Accent colour — five choices, applied across the app.
  • Text size and density — scale the whole interface.

General

  • Language — system, English or Hindi.
  • Notifications — tips and release announcements.
  • Full-screen mode — hide the status and navigation bars while using Camara.
  • Mirror selfie — save front-camera photos the way you see them.
  • Grid by default — open the camera with the composition grid already on.
  • Default timer — off, 3, 5 or 10 seconds.

Features

Switch off anything you don't use and its control disappears from the camera: Filters, Stickers, Beauty, AR lenses, Portrait depth, Teleprompter.

Updates

Shows the installed version and a Check for updates button. When a newer release exists, Update now downloads the signed APK and hands it to the installer — no browser.

Diagnostics

Log collection is on by default, including uncaught crashes, so a problem can be diagnosed without reproducing it first. You can share the log, export it as .txt, .json, .md or .zip, or clear it. Turning collection off stops new entries but still lets you export what exists.

Reset

Reset every setting back to its default.

How it is built

  • Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, CameraX for capture, Media3 for video.
  • On-device machine learning — face detection and subject segmentation run on the phone. Photos are not uploaded anywhere.
  • Small on purpose — the ML models live in Google Play Services and download on first use, which keeps the APK around 5 MB instead of 80 MB. Face lenses, face beauty and portrait depth need Play Services as a result; everything else works offline.
  • Signed releases — every build is signed with the same key, so updates install in place.

Requirements and known limits

  • Android 7.0 (API 24) or newer. arm64 and armeabi-v7a devices.
  • Face lenses, face beauty and portrait depth need Google Play Services. The app tells you when they aren't available instead of failing silently.
  • The live look applies to the preview and to saved photos; it is not yet baked into recorded video.
  • The video editor does not yet do text overlays, stickers or transitions.
